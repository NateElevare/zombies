# Material generation

Both source images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool, opaque background. No API/CLI fallback was used. `house-materials.png` and `sedan-materials.png` are the original outputs; the game projects cropped material regions onto separate 2D prop faces. Characters extend the existing native rig and animation baker, rather than using generated sheets with inconsistent frame registration.

## House

Game texture asset, stylized-concept. Create a precisely aligned 2 by 2 square texture atlas for ONE abandoned small American bungalow, gritty detailed pixel art matching a muted zombie survival game, restricted earthy palette, crisp pixel clusters. Image size square. Each quadrant fills exactly one quarter edge to edge; NO gaps, labels, borders, perspective or scene. Upper left quadrant: flat orthographic straight-on exterior wall elevation, faded cream horizontal clapboard siding, one centered dark teal sash window with chipped white trim, partly cracked glass and curtains, red brick foundation across bottom 15 percent, moss and peeling paint. Upper right quadrant: just continuous faded cream clapboard siding with weathering, brick foundation across bottom 15 percent, no window or door. Lower left quadrant: seamless flat overhead charcoal gray asphalt roof shingles with subtle moss flecks, fill the square. Lower right quadrant: flat overhead worn warm brown wooden floorboards, fill the square, no furniture. All four quadrants are material/elevation textures for mapping to game geometry, uniform flat lighting, no cast shadows, no text.

## Sedan

Game texture asset, stylized-concept. Create a precise 2 by 2 square texture atlas of ONE weathered dark desaturated teal blue 1980s four-door sedan, gritty detailed pixel art, limited colors, crisp pixel clusters, rust and scratches. Every quadrant fills exactly a quarter; NO gaps, text, labels, borders, floor, drop shadows or perspective. Upper left quadrant: perfectly flat side elevation vehicle BODY PANEL from front to rear, front at LEFT, stretched full width and height: rusty blue lower doors and fenders, chrome handles, panel seams, wheel arches at 22% and 78% width along bottom, dark tires with steel hubcaps. Do not include cabin roof or windows in this quadrant; only lower body from beltline to tires. Upper right quadrant: perfectly flat overhead hood panel, full square blue paint, subtle parallel pressed metal ridges and chipped paint rust mainly around edges. Lower left quadrant: perfectly flat side cabin elevation with dark windows, thin blue pillars and thin chrome trim, fill edge to edge: front windshield at LEFT, two large side windows and tiny rear quarter window. Lower right quadrant: flat overhead roof panel, blue paint with scratches, dust and subtle patina, full square. Same vehicle color across all panels, flat diffuse lighting. These are UV texture panels to map onto a game car's geometry, not finished perspective car illustrations.
